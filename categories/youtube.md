# YouTube Growth

| Umbrella repo | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`open-ai-youtube-agent`](https://github.com/SamurAIGPT/open-ai-youtube-agent) | Video SEO (titles/descriptions/tags/thumbnails), keyword & tag research, competitor channel analysis, trend discovery, channel analytics — the VidIQ-style toolkit | Blueprint |

Video SEO Optimization is live today, built entirely on capabilities already shipped elsewhere in this catalog (`ai-image-agent` for thumbnails, `ai-seo-agent` for a keyword-data proxy, `ai-social-agent` for the actual YouTube upload). Keyword & Tag Research, Competitor Channel Analysis, and Trend Discovery are also now Blueprint — Muapi's SEO provider (DataForSEO) has its YouTube SERP API family wired up (Organic search rankings, Video Info, Subtitles, Comments). Only Channel Analytics remains Coming Soon — it needs the channel owner's own private, OAuth-gated YouTube Analytics data, which no third-party marketplace can proxy.
